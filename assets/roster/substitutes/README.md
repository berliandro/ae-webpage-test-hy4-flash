## Art direction

`*-pixel.png` are derived locally from the originals: consistent head-and-shoulders crop,
resize, palette mapping to colours sampled from `assets/poker-arena/background.png`,
ordered dithering, and nearest-neighbour pixel scaling. Faces remain recognisable and the
pixel density matches the arena artwork. Originals are retained unmodified in this folder.

No paid licences were purchased. No Instagram images were downloaded.
