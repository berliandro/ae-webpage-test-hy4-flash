The road here

Verified history
2017

The organisation begins

Alter Ego is recorded as an Indonesian esports organisation founded in 2017. The exact day and month are not published.

2018

Into Mobile Legends

The MLBB team dates to 21 May 2018. Alter Ego entered the MPL Indonesia Season 2 qualifier but did not reach the regular season.

2019

First MPL seasons

Qualified through the closed qualifiers for Season 3 and finished 5th–6th. Reached third place in Season 4.

2020

MPL Invitational champions

Won the ONE Esports MPL Invitational, sweeping Bren Esports 3–0 in the final. Later finished fourth at the M2 World Championship.

2026

M7 runner-up, then Season 18

Reached the M7 World Championship final in January, losing 0–4 to Aurora Gaming PH. Finished third in the Season 18 regular table and fourth after playoffs in November.

About this team

Alter Ego is an Indonesian esports organisation fielding an all-Indonesian Mobile Legends: Bang Bang team. Its MLBB division has competed in MPL Indonesia since 2018 and has appeared at multiple world championships.

The tagline displayed on the team's wiki profile is #ALTERCHAMP. This site uses it as a visual motif; it is not presented here as a formally registered slogan.

For the current roster, staff and season results, see Roster and Competition.
