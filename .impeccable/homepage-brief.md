# Homepage — surface brief

## Scope and visitor mode

- Surface: homepage `/` (Experience mode — the artifact leads, the interface recedes).
- Audience: Alter Ego / MPL ID fans first, legible to newcomers. The visitor arrives to feel personally seated at a high-stakes table holding the team in their hand.
- Job: believe, for one viewport, that they are physically holding five oversized cards at a poker table.
- Real content: five verified core players — Nino, Affan, Dalvin, Dingarai, Alexander — each with an official MPL role. No invented stats, history, or ranks. Player-to-rank assignment is a creative proposal, not a roster fact, and stays undecided.

## Outcome and proof

- The cards and hands are the proof. The arena (opponents, felt, chips, banner, architecture) is the supporting world.
- Source truth: pinned poker-table concept and the pixel-art arena reference image (`Retro Pixel Art Poker Arena.png`). All five cards are complete, text-free pixel-art layers; the background is the arena; hands are separate transparent cutouts.
- Success: a visitor who leaves after one viewport describes "holding Alter Ego's cards at a poker table," not a website.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

- World: retro pixel-art poker arena — dark blue-charcoal architecture, green felt, crimson ornamental splatter on cream cards, amber overhead light, warm skin, red-orange jersey stripes. Pinned by the user; no alternative world explored.
- Hero moment: the five-card fan, sharp and lit, held by two hands entering from the bottom, with the arena softly blurred and darkened behind.
- Signature interaction: restrained, physically plausible card lift within the fan on hover/focus/tap, revealing a quiet name + role label. Restrained ambient hand breath. Static and fully legible under reduced motion.

## Unresolved decisions

- Player → rank creative mapping (undecided by instruction; labels use display names + roles only).
- Whether substitute-card interaction is built.
- Supporting content ceiling and any sections below the hero.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The viewport is the hand itself, not a hero containing a hand. Conventional site chrome and navigation do not enter the first viewport; the arena exists to prove the cards are physically held.

OWN-WORLD: Pixel-art arena — charcoal/teal felt, crimson splatter, cream card stock, amber pool light, hard pixel edges, 1:1 pixel grid at 1672×941, family density locked. The world is the illustration itself, not a theme over a template.

STORY: The visitor understands they hold Alter Ego's five players; they believe the table and opponents are real; they can inspect a card without leaving the hand.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed 16:9 arena, softly blurred and darkened; five sharp fanned cards in the lower two-thirds at their exact source offsets; two hands gripping from the bottom edge; small Alter Ego wordmark high in the dark; one quiet hint line.

FORM: Single immersive hero. Position on the ordered list: pinned whole by the user's brief ("cinematic poker/card-game concept", "first-person POV of someone holding five playing cards"). seed: none (pinned).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
